Universal Beat

Everyone hears the same loop. You get to change it once.

There is one sixteen-step loop and everybody who opens this page hears the same version of it.

You can change it once. Add a note, retune a note, or delete a note. After that you can only listen. Whatever you delete was put there by someone else, and whatever you add can be deleted by the next person who shows up.
