Thesis - 001 2025

Humanoid Skating

2025
MSC THESIS - ETHZ IDSC

RL-trained humanoid on inline skates fitted to a Booster T1. Custom Gym environment, realistic wheel dynamics, tuned reward shaping. Deployed sim-to-sim and sim-to-real, faster, more efficient, and more robust than prior humanoid-skater work, with no motion priors and no imitation learning.

Reinforcement Learning Isaac Sim-to-Real Locomotion Humanoids
Industry - 002 2024 to 2025

Warehouse Pallet Tracking

2024
ML & Insights Intern - Verity

Repurposed Verity's warehouse drone hardware and CV stack to track forklifts and register pallet moves. Field-deployed a prototype at a client facility, gathered reliability metrics, and built a simulated warehouse to stress-test failure modes. The work made the case for a hybrid drone-plus-forklift tracking system.

Computer Vision Drones Embedded Systems Hardware Design
Lab - 003 2023 to 2024

11-DoF Bio-Mimetic Hand

2023
REAL WORLD ROBOTICS - ETHZ SOFT ROBOTICS LAB

Designed a custom 11-DoF bio-mimetic hand: CAD, electronics, and digital twin model. Trained an RL policy in Isaac Gym to roll a ball about arbitrary axes at a target velocity. Deployed successfully on the physical hand, which could also be actuated via teleoperation.

Autonomous Maze Solver

2023
SENIOR DESIGN - NEW YORK UNIVERSITY

A tilt-actuated marble maze with computer-vision path planning. Custom CAD and mechatronics for the two-axis tilt rig; a vision pipeline segmented the maze and computed a path for the marble to follow. Awarded NYU Tandon's 2023 Best Senior Design.
